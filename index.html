About B&W Auto Repair

At B&W Auto Repair LLC, we are more than just a repair shop — we are your neighbors. Located in the heart of Central New Jersey, our family-owned business has been serving the community with honest, reliable, and affordable auto repair services.

Our team of certified mechanics brings years of experience and a genuine passion for keeping your vehicle running safely and smoothly. We treat every car as if it were our own.
