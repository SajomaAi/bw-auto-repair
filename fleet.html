WHY B&W

WORK MOST SHOPS TURN AWAY

Fleet vehicles are not big cars. They need capabilities a general repair shop usually sends somewhere else.

DIESEL & MEDIUM-DUTY

Diesel service, and tires up to 19.5" LT. Box trucks, buses, and heavy vans get worked on here instead of being sent out.

TOWING & RECOVERY

We recover the vehicle ourselves and bring it straight to our bay. One call, one company, no waiting on a third-party tow before the diagnosis even starts.

WELDING & FABRICATION

Frames, brackets, exhaust, and custom fabrication. Fleet vehicles break in ways a parts-swapper cannot fix — we make the part when there isn't one.

BODY & PAINT

Dents, rust, panel and roof repair, and refinishing. Vehicles carrying your name on the door should look like it.

FULL MECHANICAL

Engine and transmission, brakes, suspension, electrical diagnostics, A/C, exhaust and emissions. The whole vehicle, one shop, one invoice.

BILINGUAL SHOP
